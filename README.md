# Ethics Under Attack 💼💻🌱👨‍💼

Inspired by the fast-paced gameplay of **Organ Attack**, **Ethics Under Attack** is a 4-player educational card game that challenges players to protect their engineering company's ethical systems while sabotaging their opponents using unethical practices inspired by **Global Issues in Engineering Ethics**.

## 🎮 About the Game

Each player represents an engineering organization with four core ethical systems:

- 💼 Business Ethics
- 💻 Computer Ethics
- 🌱 Environmental Ethics
- 👨‍💼 Engineering Management

Players must strategically use **Attack Cards**, **Defense Cards**, **Repair Cards**, **Special/Event Cards**, and **Ethics Policy Cards** to protect their organization's ethical foundations while eliminating their opponents.

The last player with at least one remaining Ethics Card wins.

## 🃏 Game Overview

- **Players:** 4
- **Recommended Age:** 16+
- **Playing Time:** 20–30 minutes
- **Educational Focus:** Global Issues in Engineering Ethics

## 📦 Game Components

| Component | Quantity |
|-----------|---------:|
| Ethics Cards | 16 |

| **Total Cards** | **92** |

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

Ethics Under Attack is designed to help players:

- Understand the importance of engineering ethics.
- Recognize common ethical issues faced by engineers.
- Apply ethical decision-making in realistic scenarios.
- Learn concepts related to Business Ethics, Computer Ethics, Environmental Ethics, and Engineering Management.
- Explore the **Ten Commandments of Computer Ethics** through interactive gameplay.

## 📜 Inspiration

The game's mechanics are inspired by **Organ Attack**, while its educational content is based on the **Global Issues in Engineering Ethics** module, transforming engineering ethics concepts into an engaging and competitive tabletop experience.

---

## 👥 Creators

**Ethics Under Attack** was designed and developed by **3rd Year Mechatronics Engineering students** as a final requirement for the course:

**MExE 410 – Codes, Standards and Professional Ethics for Mechatronics Engineering**

This project transforms engineering ethics concepts into an engaging educational card game that promotes strategic thinking, ethical decision-making, and collaborative learning through interactive gameplay.
