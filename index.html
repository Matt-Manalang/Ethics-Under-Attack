Introduction

What is this?

Ethics Under Attack is a 4-player educational card game based on Global Issues in Engineering Ethics. Each player protects four Ethics Cards representing core professional values — while attacking to break down opponents' values with real-world ethical failures.

The Goal

Destroy your opponents' Ethics Cards using matched Attack cards, defend your own with Defense cards, and repair the damage when you can. Outlast the table to win.

Game Setup

Each player places four Ethics Cards face-up: Business, Computer, Environmental, and Engineering Management.
Shuffle the 64-card Main Deck and deal 5 cards to each player.
Shuffle the 10-card Ethics Policy Deck and reveal the top card. It becomes the active policy.
The remaining Main Deck becomes the Draw Deck. You're ready to play.

The Four Card Types

click a card to flip it over

⚡

Attack

12 unique · 24 cardsTAP TO FLIP
How it works

Each Attack card destroys its matching Ethics Card — unless blocked by a Defense card. 2 copies of each of the 12 attacks.

🛡

Defense

8 unique · 16 cardsTAP TO FLIP
How it works

Played only as a reaction to an attack. The Code of Ethics defense blocks any attack. 2 copies of each of the 8 defenses.

🔧

Repair

4 unique · 8 cardsTAP TO FLIP
How it works

Security Update, Environmental Cleanup, Ethics Seminar, and Leadership Workshop each repair the matching Ethics Card. 2 copies each.

★

Special / Event

7 unique · 18 cardsTAP TO FLIP
How it works

Government Audit, Whistleblower, Budget Increase, Public Complaint, Emergency Meeting, and ISO Certification (2 copies each), plus Revised Code of Ethics (4 copies) — which replaces the active Ethics Policy.

🟨 Special / Event Cards
Government Audit
Duration: Immediate

Choose one opponent — they discard 2 random cards from their hand. If the Active Ethics Policy modifies this card, follow the policy's effect instead.

ISO Certification
Duration: Immediate

Repair any one of your destroyed Ethics Cards by flipping it face-up — any type, regardless of category.

Whistleblower
Duration: Immediate

Look at one opponent's hand, choose one card, and add it to your own. If the Active Ethics Policy modifies this card, follow the policy's effect instead.

Public Complaint
Duration: One full turn

Choose one opponent — they cannot play Attack Cards on their next turn. They may still draw, play Repair or Special/Event cards, and use Defense cards if attacked.

Budget Increase
Duration: Immediate

Draw 2 additional cards from the Main Deck immediately, added to your hand.

Emergency Meeting
Duration: Immediate

Every player discards their entire hand, then immediately draws 5 new cards. Ethics Cards, the Active Ethics Policy, and the Draw/Discard Piles are unaffected (except for the discarded hands).

Multinational Expansion
Duration: Immediate

Draw 2 cards, then choose one: repair 1 Ethics Card, or play 1 additional Attack Card this turn.

Revised Code of Ethics
Duration: Immediate (new policy stays active until replaced)

Discard the current Active Ethics Policy, then reveal the top card of the Ethics Policy Deck — it immediately becomes the new Active Ethics Policy. If the Policy Deck runs out, reshuffle the discarded policies into a new deck.

Heads up — this list includes Multinational Expansion, which brings Special/Event Cards to 8 unique designs. The Card Types overview above (and the component tally) still shows 7 unique / 18 cards — let me know the intended print quantity for Multinational Expansion and I'll update those counts to match.

🟪 Ethics Policy Cards
Do Not Use a Computer to Harm Other People
Duration: Until replaced

A player cannot target the same opponent with an Attack Card on two consecutive turns. If they try, the Attack Card is discarded without effect and their turn ends immediately.

Do Not Interfere with Other People's Work
Duration: Until replaced

A player may not attack the same opponent on consecutive turns — they must target someone else first. Violating attacks fail and are discarded.

Do Not Snoop Around in Other People's Files
Duration: Until replaced

Whistleblower may only look at an opponent's hand — it cannot steal a card. Government Audit lets the player look at the opponent's hand and shuffle one chosen card back into the Main Deck, instead of discarding two random cards.

Do Not Use a Computer to Steal
Duration: Until replaced

Whistleblower and Intellectual Property Theft cannot be played — either card is immediately discarded without effect if attempted.

Do Not Bear False Witness
Duration: Until replaced

Fraudulent Report no longer destroys Business Ethics. Instead, the targeted player skips their Draw Phase on their next turn.

Do Not Copy Software You Did Not Pay For
Duration: Until replaced

Data Breach and Hacking become temporary — instead of destroying Computer Ethics, the card is turned sideways and automatically returns face-up at the start of the owner's next turn.

Do Not Use Other People's Intellectual Output
Duration: Until replaced

If Intellectual Property Theft is blocked by a Defense Card, the attacking player must discard 2 additional cards from their hand.

Think About the Social Consequences of the Program You Are Writing
Duration: Until replaced

Whenever a player successfully destroys an opponent's Ethics Card, they must immediately discard 1 random card from their own hand.

Always Consider the Public Good
Duration: Until replaced

At the start of their turn, each player chooses one: draw 1 card, or repair 1 destroyed Ethics Card for free — not both.

Use Computers with Consideration and Respect
Duration: Until replaced

Before an Attack Card resolves, the targeted player may discard 1 card from their hand. If they do, the attacker must choose a different opponent or end their turn without attacking.

  3. Do not snoop around other people's files
  4. Do not use a computer to steal
  5. Do not bear false witness
  6. Do not copy software you did not pay for
  7. Respect other people's intellectual output
  8. Think about the social consequences
  9. Always consider the public good
  10. Use computers with consideration and respect

Winning & Losing

A player is eliminated when all four of their Ethics Cards are destroyed. The last player remaining wins the game. 🏆
